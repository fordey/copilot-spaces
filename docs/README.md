# Acme Project Management Docs

A central index for Acme's project management guidance.

## Project management processes at a glance
Acme manages work through an iterative lifecycle: initiate and validate an idea, align stakeholders and define outcomes, plan scope, milestones, backlog, dependencies, and risks, then execute in small increments with regular standups, delivery syncs, reviews, testing, and progress tracking. Teams manage risks and communicate status throughout delivery, release with readiness checks and post-deployment verification, and close the loop through retrospectives and tracked improvement actions. Clear ownership, customer value, measurable outcomes, and psychological safety guide the process.

## How these docs fit together
Acme's process model begins with initiation and stakeholder alignment, moves into planning and backlog definition, then proceeds through execution with regular tracking and quality checks. As work progresses, the team monitors risk, communicates status, and prepares for release. After delivery, the team reflects on outcomes and converts lessons into improvement actions so the next cycle starts from a stronger baseline.
